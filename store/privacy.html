Click Clack Privacy Policy

Last updated September 14, 2026

Click Clack is a daily question app for two people. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address and a user ID from Google sign-in.
  • What you put in the app: answers, moods, reactions, messages, photos you add on special days, decks and profile results.
  • Details you choose to add: birthday, gender, relationship dates and a ZIP code.
  • A push token so we can send notifications.

How it's used

Only to run the app: to pick the day's question, show your answers to your partner once you've both answered, mark special days, show local weather from your ZIP code, and send notifications. We do not show ads, sell data, or use it for advertising or tracking.

Who can see it

Your content is visible to you and the one person you pair with. Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Messaging) and is encrypted in transit. Weather comes from the U.S. National Weather Service using your ZIP code's location; no personal details are sent with it.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept while your account exists. Unpairing ends a pairing for both of you but does not delete it. To delete your account and data, email justin.dial@mawdpathology.com from the address you signed in with, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

Click Clack is for adults 18 and over and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change too.
