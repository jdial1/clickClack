Delete your Click Clack account

Sign in with the Google account you use in Click Clack, then confirm. This can't be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile: name, birthday, gender, ZIP code and settings
  • Your answers, deck runs and test results
  • Messages, photos, dates, bucket list items and questions you added

What happens to your partner

Your pairing ends. Your partner keeps their own answers, but can no longer see yours. When both of you have deleted your accounts, everything left from the pairing is deleted too.
